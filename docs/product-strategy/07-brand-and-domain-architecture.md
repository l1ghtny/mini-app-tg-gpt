# Brand and domain architecture

## Problem with the current name

“AIwithUI” can remain a historical Telegram identifier, but it is weak as a web master brand:

- every modern AI product has a user interface, so the phrase does not differentiate;
- it is awkward to pronounce, remember, and type for Russian speakers;
- capitalization and word boundaries are ambiguous;
- it anchors the story to implementation rather than an outcome;
- it does not naturally support Russian branded search.

Renaming the visible product does not require abandoning the Telegram username or existing referral links.

## Recommended direction

Use **Lightny AI** initially, with the option to simplify to **Lightny** after brand recognition grows. It is distinctive, connected to the existing operator/project identity, and flexible enough to cover research, documents, and future business products.

Suggested architecture:

- `lightny.ru` — canonical Russian marketing, SEO, pricing, help, and legal site;
- `app.lightny.ru` — browser application;
- `api.lightny.ru` — public/backend API boundary;
- `t.me/AIwithUIbot` — existing Telegram distribution and continuity;
- `lightny.ai` — defensive registration and redirect if it is available and worth the renewal cost.

At the July 2026 check, `lightny.ru` appeared registered through Timeweb and paid through 2027-05-28, while `lightny.ai` appeared unregistered. This is not proof of ownership or purchasability. Confirm registrar access, trademark risk, renewal pricing, and live availability before deciding.

## Why `.ru` should be primary

- It immediately signals a Russian-language service and familiar commercial context.
- It is easier to remember for the initial market.
- It separates the product from generic AI-domain naming.
- The `.ai` domain can redirect and protect the name without fragmenting SEO authority.

Use a single canonical domain. Do not publish equivalent indexable marketing sites on both `.ru` and `.ai`.

## Migration sequence

1. Confirm ownership and renewal control of `lightny.ru`.
2. Run a legal/trademark and social-handle check before committing to visual identity.
3. Reserve defensive domains and key handles only after checking current availability and renewal costs.
4. Define the visible relationship: “Lightny AI” product; “AIwithUI” legacy bot username.
5. Configure DNS, TLS, email authentication, analytics ownership, and uptime monitoring.
6. Set canonical URLs, redirects, sitemap, robots, structured data, and Open Graph assets.
7. Update the Telegram display name and description without changing the username or breaking deep links.
8. Announce the change inside the product and preserve old branded search pages for an appropriate transition period.

## Naming alternatives

If Lightny fails legal or customer testing, evaluate names against these criteria:

- pronounceable in Russian and English;
- distinctive in search;
- no required hyphen or unusual capitalization;
- credible for individual and business use;
- domain and Telegram/social availability;
- no narrow tie to a single model or delivery surface;
- acceptable trademark risk.

Do not select a name solely because a cheap domain is available.

## Brand validation

Before investing in a full identity, test three names and descriptors with at least 15 target customers. Ask them to recall the name after a delay, pronounce it, describe what they think the product does, and choose it from a search-results mockup. Measure unaided recall and category comprehension instead of preference alone.

## Initial descriptor

The brand should nearly always appear with a descriptor during the transition. Candidate direction:

> Lightny AI — GPT, Gemini, and AI search in one workspace.

The final public Russian descriptor should be edited as native Russian and tested against the primary customer segment.
