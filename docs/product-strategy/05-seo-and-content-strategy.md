# SEO and content strategy

## Objective

Organic acquisition should bring users with a specific job to a page that proves the workflow and transfers the task into the application. Page count is not the goal. The unit of SEO value is a page with original evidence, a clear intent, and a measurable product handoff.

## Technical foundation

### Domain and indexing

- Move from the temporary Lovable hostname to the canonical branded domain.
- Use one canonical origin consistently in metadata, sitemap, robots, structured data, Open Graph, and internal links.
- Set the production `SITE_URL` so the environment-aware sitemap, robots response, canonicals, and social URLs stop falling back to the temporary Lovable hostname.
- Add Google Search Console, Yandex Webmaster, and Yandex Metrica; verify ownership and sitemap processing.
- Include accurate `lastmod` values in the sitemap.
- Preserve useful legacy `-v-telegram` URLs with intentional redirects or canonicals when creating web-first routes.
- Monitor index coverage, duplicate titles, canonical conflicts, and query cannibalization.

### Metadata and presentation

- Create a real logo, favicon set, and share image.
- Add per-page Open Graph and social metadata.
- Use `Organization` and `SoftwareApplication` or `WebApplication` structured data where facts are verifiable.
- Add `Offer` data only when public prices are current and directly available on the page.
- Never fabricate rating or review structured data.

`llms.txt` can remain as a low-cost machine-readable summary, but it should not be treated as a Google ranking lever. FAQ content should serve users even though FAQ rich results are no longer a general growth tactic.

## Search-intent clusters

### 1. Category and access

- AI/neural-network aggregator online;
- GPT, Gemini, and Claude access in Russia;
- AI tools without foreign cards;
- browser versus Telegram AI assistant.

### 2. Research and sources

- AI search with sources;
- how to verify AI citations;
- Perplexity alternatives and comparisons;
- research workflows for analysts, marketers, and students.

### 3. Documents

- analyze, summarize, compare, and question PDF files;
- extract tables and facts from documents;
- compare contracts or reports with appropriate legal disclaimers;
- work with several documents and export results.

### 4. Models and comparisons

- GPT versus Gemini versus Claude for a specific Russian-language task;
- model cost, context, speed, citation, and file-handling comparisons;
- release notes and repeatable monthly benchmarks.

### 5. Role workflows

- market research for a small business;
- content research and brief creation;
- code and technical-documentation work;
- study and explanation workflows with academic-integrity guidance.

### 6. Images

- generate and edit images for specific commercial tasks;
- same-prompt model comparisons;
- size, quality, text rendering, and cost tests.

## Content moat: original evidence

Generic summaries of AI features are easy to reproduce and unlikely to earn durable authority. Build reusable test suites and publish the methodology:

- the same Russian tasks across GPT, Gemini, Claude, and Sonar;
- cost per completed task, not merely token price;
- PDF question-answer tests with citation accuracy and failure cases;
- source-quality evaluation for AI search;
- Russian writing and instruction-following benchmarks;
- identical-prompt image comparisons;
- monthly catalogue, latency, and reliability reports;
- customer case studies with permission and measurable before/after outcomes.

Each benchmark should show the date, model version, prompt, settings, evaluation rubric, failures, and relevant commercial relationship.

## Page template requirements

Every search page should have:

- a unique intent and title;
- a concise direct answer;
- a real workflow or test;
- screenshots or outputs where useful;
- limitations and update date;
- internal links to the parent cluster and related workflows;
- a CTA that carries the relevant prompt/template into the app;
- an author or editorial responsibility signal where appropriate.

Avoid mass-producing near-identical city, profession, or model pages. If two pages answer the same query with the same evidence, consolidate them.

## Measurement

Track by landing page and query cluster:

- impressions, ranking distribution, and click-through rate;
- engaged sessions and scroll/proof interaction;
- CTA clicks and successful prompt handoffs;
- authentication completion;
- first completed task;
- trial-to-paid conversion and retained paid users;
- assisted conversions, because educational content may not convert in one session.

Traffic without activation is not success.
