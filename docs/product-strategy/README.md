# AI with UI / Lightny product strategy

Last updated: 2026-07-26

This folder is the durable strategy record for turning the Telegram-first product into a browser-first AI workspace that also works exceptionally well in Telegram. It covers the market position, product gaps, landing and SEO work, trust requirements, brand and domain options, metrics, and a sequenced execution plan.

The recommendations are based on the current backend, frontend, and SEO repositories plus a July 2026 review of Russian AI aggregators. They are strategic recommendations, not verified legal advice. Product claims, prices, model availability, payment methods, and data-handling statements must always be checked against production before publication.

## Recommended direction

Do not compete primarily on the number of models. The defensible position is:

> A focused AI workspace for research, documents, writing, and code — with GPT, Gemini, and AI search, available in the browser and Telegram.

The browser should be the primary acquisition, activation, and payment surface. Telegram should be the fast companion: it preserves continuity, notifications, sharing, and access when a full browser workflow is unnecessary.

The strongest wedge is **research-to-result continuity**:

- reliable AI search with sources;
- work with files and documents;
- a curated model set instead of a confusing model directory;
- the same useful workspace across browser and Telegram;
- transparent limits, costs, and model selection.

## How to use this pack

- Treat `08-execution-roadmap.md` as the operating backlog.
- Use `14-work-mvp-completion-plan.md` as the acceptance gate for Work; its
  evaluation evidence takes precedence over speculative Work expansion.
- Review `06-trust-legal-and-launch-readiness.md` before making public claims or taking payments on the web.
- Use `05-seo-and-content-strategy.md` when creating new SEO routes; avoid publishing generic pages merely to increase page count.
- Revisit the competitor facts and prices quarterly because they change quickly.
- Record material product or positioning decisions in project memory and update these documents when a recommendation is accepted or rejected.
