# Positioning and niche

## Market reality

Russian AI aggregators have made several benefits into table stakes: access without foreign cards, familiar payment methods, multiple model providers, image generation, a free starting allowance, and a simple Russian interface. Large competitors can credibly advertise dozens or hundreds of models. Matching that catalogue is expensive and does not create a clear reason to choose AI with UI.

The product already has a more valuable foundation than its public story suggests: resilient chat streaming, search modes, document workflows, images, conversations and folders, a real entitlement system, browser authentication work, Telegram distribution, and a curated GPT/Gemini/Perplexity catalogue.

## Recommended niche

### Category

**AI workspace for research and everyday knowledge work.**

### One-sentence position

> An AI workspace for research and everyday work without token math: choose the level of intelligence, get a completed answer for one clear request, and continue in the browser or Telegram.

### Russian customer-facing direction

Final Russian copy should receive a native-language review. Candidate directions:

- «Lightny AI — GPT, Gemini и AI-поиск в одном рабочем пространстве»
- «Lightny AI — нейросети для работы в браузере и Telegram»
- «Находите источники, разбирайте документы и доводите задачи до результата»

These are positioning prompts, not final approved headlines.

## Ideal initial customers

Focus on customers with recurring text- and research-heavy work rather than everyone who has heard about AI.

1. Researchers, analysts, and managers who need answers with sources and summaries from documents.
2. Marketers and small business owners who research markets and turn findings into publishable text.
3. Developers and technical specialists who compare approaches, inspect documentation, and work with code.
4. Students and educators who need explanations and document analysis, with clear academic-integrity guidance.

The first three groups are likelier to pay for saved time. Student traffic can be valuable for SEO and referrals, but it should not define the entire product.

## Jobs to be done

- “Find reliable information and show me where it came from.”
- “Read these files, compare them, and produce a useful result.”
- “Choose the right model for my task without making me study model benchmarks.”
- “Continue the same task from my computer or Telegram.”
- “Tell me what this request will cost before I run it.”
- “Turn the result into something I can export, share, or reuse.”

## Differentiation pillars

### 1. Research-to-result

Search is not merely another toggle. The experience should guide a user from a sourced answer through document analysis to a reusable output. Citations, source quality, and export are central.

### 2. Curated intelligence

The product should recommend a model or mode based on the task, explain the choice in plain language, show which request pool it uses, and still let advanced users override it.

### 3. Cross-surface continuity

The web application is the primary workspace. Telegram is the companion for quick questions, notifications, shared links, and continuation. Account linking and shared history must be verified end to end before this is advertised as seamless.

### 4. Honest economics

Show exact prices, request allowances, renewal rules, and top-up expiration. For text, one completed answer should consume one clearly identified request. Transparency can be a differentiator in a market full of tokens, credits, Caps, sparks, and model multipliers.

## What not to lead with

- “All neural networks in one place.” It is generic and invites a catalogue-size comparison.
- “100+ tools” until the product genuinely has them and users can understand why they matter.
- Being inside Telegram. Telegram is a delivery surface, not the product category.
- “AI with UI.” It describes a basic property of every competitor and is difficult to pronounce or search for in Russian.
- Cheapness alone. A price war is easy to copy and can damage inference margins.
- Video and music generation before the research/document core is excellent.

## Positioning test

The position is working when target customers can answer all three questions after seeing the first screen for five seconds:

1. What is it? An AI workspace with leading models and search.
2. What can I accomplish? Research, understand files, write, and code.
3. Why this one? It chooses the right tool, shows sources and clear request limits, and continues across web and Telegram.
