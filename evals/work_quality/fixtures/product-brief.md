# Atlas beta product brief

Atlas helps small operations teams turn support tickets into a prioritized weekly improvement list. The beta is intended for teams of 5–30 people.

## Current evidence

- Seven design partners use the prototype weekly.
- Four partners asked for a way to connect decisions back to the original ticket.
- Median weekly active use is 3 sessions per participating team.
- The product does not yet support role-based access or data export.

## Beta objective

Learn whether a shared, evidence-linked improvement list saves team leads at least two hours per week without creating trust concerns.
