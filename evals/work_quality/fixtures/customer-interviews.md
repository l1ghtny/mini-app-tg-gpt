# Atlas interview notes

## Northwind — operations lead

The team spends Monday morning copying ticket excerpts into a planning document. The lead wants every recommendation to link back to the ticket and would not use automatic prioritization without an explanation.

## Contoso — support manager

The current prototype is useful for grouping repeated complaints. The manager needs CSV export before involving the product team and worries that contractors could see restricted tickets.

## Tailspin — founder

The founder values a short weekly summary and wants to know whether the tool actually reduces planning time. They can tolerate manual export during beta but need clear deletion controls.
